Detection × reasoning: one framework, no silent decisions

Omics tables arrive in dozens of shapes. PRISM identifies the structure deterministically wherever it can, asks an AI only when it can't, and never acts on a guess without your confirmation.

01

Deterministic first

Known exports are matched by exact column signatures. A match is certain, so no language model is ever called for it.

02

AI proposes, you decide

For unknown layouts, every AI-proposed column role is shown with its confidence and evidence, and must be confirmed or corrected.

03

Nothing is transformed

Recognition only labels structure. No value is filtered, imputed, normalized or rewritten at this stage.

04

Everything is logged

Each proposal and each confirmation is timestamped with the original and final value, so a session can be fully reconstructed.
